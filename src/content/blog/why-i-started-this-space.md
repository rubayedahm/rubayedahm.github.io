For a while I've been doing a lot of things at once: studying Business Management and Leadership, leading IT at IHRS, working as an administrative assistant, and representing my course mates as a course rep. It's a lot of learning, but most of it disappears into notes apps and late-night thoughts.

So I'm giving it a home.

## What this is (and what it isn't)

This isn't a place for essays or assignments. It's closer to a work journal, written in public. I'll share:

- **Projects** I'm working on, including the messy middle and not just the finished result
- **Lessons** from work, university and life, especially the ones I learned the hard way
- **Skills** I'm building, and what actually helped me get better at them

> The goal isn't to look like I have everything figured out. It's to show the work and how I think.

## Why write in public?

Writing things down forces me to think more clearly. If I can't explain something simply, I probably don't understand it yet. And sharing it means someone else might find it useful, or tell me where I'm wrong, which is just as valuable.

It also keeps me honest. It's easy to say you're "working on something". It's harder to show it.

## What's next

I'll be posting whenever I have something worth sharing, whether that's a small win, a mistake, or something new I've picked up. If anything here is useful to you, or you just want to say hello, my [contact form](/#contact) is always open.

Thanks for reading. More soon.
